When judging the set needs what a carried member was judged to say, run `show-audit` with this principle's id, which
prints that member's stored assessment. A carried member here shows only its `assessed_at`, the facts that say what
moved around it, and any check, whose `check_result` its template still asks for, unless one of those facts fires: then
it prints whole, with its stored assessment and evidence. It prints whole when `evidence_changed` or
`file_changed_outside_member` is true, when a `mapped_elsewhere` entry names `open_finding_ids`, and when this principle
leaves a finding open on it: its `open_finding_ids` names that finding, the same one the prior findings list. An
observed delivery's `observed_at` stays, since it says how old its sighting is: judge the member again when that
sighting is too old to stand behind. Replace a marker with a fresh assessment whenever you no longer stand behind the
one carried.
