add up to a changed meaning that no single step showed, so judge them together: read `member_lines`, the member as the

member under its old path if that diff shows its whole file as new. Assess the member against that text, not against the
last acknowledged one. Treat each `reason` as an earlier judgement to check again, not as evidence. Completing this
audit replaces the acknowledgements in the record, and git keeps their history.
