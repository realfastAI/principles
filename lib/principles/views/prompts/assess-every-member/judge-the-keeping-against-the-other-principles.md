`principles` lists each other principle with a member no audit has judged as it is now,
as `list-member-problems` reports it: `changed` or broken since an audit, or never assessed, as
`unchanged_since_mapping` or `no_baseline`. An empty list means every other principle's members are as an audit
last judged them. This principle's members are what is meant to bring such changes to an audit, so weigh each
listed change against them: where one has waited with nothing in the project carrying the step that would have
caught it, raise a finding on the member that should carry it. Each listed principle's own audit judges its
change; this one does not.
